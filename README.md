# Social Media & Web Analytics – Mirsha Jewelry

A data-driven social media and web analytics project conducted for Mirsha Jewelry to evaluate digital performance, user behavior, website traffic, and social media effectiveness.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes how Mirsha Jewelry's Instagram and TikTok presence contributes to website traffic, customer engagement, conversion, and retention.

The analysis combines Google Analytics 4 (GA4) data with manually collected social media performance data to identify digital performance gaps and develop actionable business recommendations.

## Business Questions

The project focused on questions such as:

- Which content types generate the highest engagement?
- How effectively does social media drive website traffic?
- Which social media visitors convert into customers?
- How can influencer campaign performance be measured?
- Which content formats provide the strongest reach?
- How can customer retention from social channels be improved?

## Data Sources

The analysis used:

- Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
- Instagram performance data
- TikTok performance data
- Instagram comments
- Competitor social media data
- Influencer and PR collaboration observations

## Analysis

### Social Media Performance

Instagram and TikTok content formats were compared to understand differences in reach and engagement.

Model-focused TikTok videos achieved significantly higher reach than product-only content, while Instagram Reels strongly outperformed static posts.

### Web Traffic Analysis

GA4 acquisition data was analyzed to understand how social media users interact with the website.

Instagram was identified as the dominant social traffic source, while landing-page analysis revealed that a large share of Instagram-driven visitors were directed to the homepage rather than relevant product pages.

### Customer Retention

New and returning visitor behavior was compared using GA4.

Returning visitors represented a small share of sessions but demonstrated substantially higher engagement, highlighting an opportunity to improve customer retention.

### Sentiment & Intent Analysis

Instagram comments were manually classified using VADER sentiment-analysis principles.

The analysis showed that most visible comments represented purchase intent, particularly price inquiries, revealing a potential information gap in the customer journey.

### Competitor Benchmarking

Mirsha's social media performance was compared with jewelry competitors to evaluate relative content reach and identify opportunities for growth.

## Key Recommendations

Based on the analysis, recommendations included:

- Increase model-focused short-form video content
- Prioritize Instagram Reels over static content
- Link Stories and social posts directly to relevant product pages
- Add UTM parameters to influencer and campaign links
- Improve pricing visibility
- Introduce retention mechanisms such as newsletter sign-ups
- Track social campaigns systematically through GA4

## Business Impact

The analysis transformed website and social media data into actionable recommendations for improving traffic quality, content strategy, campaign measurement, conversion, and customer retention.

Several findings and recommendations were presented to the company representative, who expressed interest in implementing UTM tracking, improving Instagram Story links, and testing model-focused content.
